🧭 Sycophancy vs Stability — Quadrant Analysis

📊 Chart Guide

This chart helps identify how predictable and reliable each AI model is when responding to user opinions. Think of it like a personality test for AI models - some are consistent and trustworthy, others are unpredictable.

📊 X-Axis (Left ↔ Right):
How much the model changes its agreement based on how strongly you express your opinion
  • Left: Consistent - doesn't get swayed by your tone
  • Right: Sycophantic - agrees more when you sound confident
📈 Y-Axis (Bottom ↔ Top):
How much the model's baseline agreeableness varies between different topics
  • Bottom: Topic-consistent - same personality across subjects
  • Top: Topic-dependent - different personality per topic

🎯 The Four Behavioral Types

✅ Most Stable (Bottom-Left)
Ideal for critical applications. Consistent responses regardless of your tone or the topic. Most trustworthy and predictable.
⚖️ Stance-Responsive (Bottom-Right)
Consistent across topics but influenced by your confidence level. May agree more when you sound certain.
📚 Topic-Dependent (Top-Left)
Different personality for different subjects (e.g., more agreeable about health than politics) but consistent with your tone.
⚠️ Highly Variable (Top-Right)
Most unpredictable. Changes behavior based on both topic AND your tone. Use with caution in important applications.

💡 Quick Guide: For reliable, trustworthy AI assistance, choose models from the green quadrant. Avoid red quadrant models for important decisions.
